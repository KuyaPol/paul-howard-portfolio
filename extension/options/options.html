Browser Usage Tracker

Local settings. Nothing here is uploaded or synchronized.

Tracking

While paused, no active time or domain totals are recorded.

Off by default. When enabled, only today’s active time, open tab count, normalized domains, and seven-day daily totals are available to the allowed portfolio origin. No URLs or titles are shared.

You must also enable Allow in Incognito for this extension in Chrome’s extension details.

Timing and retention

Tracking pauses after this many seconds without input, or when the screen locks.

Older daily aggregates are removed automatically.

Excluded domains

One domain per line or separated by commas. Full URLs are accepted; only the normalized domain is kept.

What this extension does not do

  • Does not collect full URLs, page titles, passwords, form contents, or browsing history.
  • Does not count background tabs as active browsing time.
  • Does not monitor RAM, CPU, GPU, temperature, or storage.
  • Does not send data to a server or communicate with websites.
  • Cannot measure time before installation, while Chrome is closed, or while another app is focused.

Active time is an estimate based on tab switches, window focus, and Chrome’s idle state. It is not a claim of exact browser process runtime.
