Concept 01 / Personal finance

Make room
for the next move.

Track income, expenses, and savings goals, then review spending and portfolio trends in one proposed dashboard.

Designed to bring the picture together.

  • Monthly income and expenses
  • Savings goals and budget limits
  • Yearly analytics and spending views
  • Portfolio tracking and market visualization
  • Optional market data or TradingView connection, proposed

Investment views are for tracking and visualization, not financial advice or automatic trading. Alerts and notifications are proposed features.
